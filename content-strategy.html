Phase 3

Content Strategy - Heather Lindsey

Phase 3 deliverable · Content pillars supporting the Inner Circle high-ticket positioning + the homepage VSL push.

Premise

Heather's ~69K @thebusytrader audience is engaged and warm, but the content currently mixes free-trade tips with motivational and faith-aligned posts. None of it routes a viewer to the new high-ticket Inner Circle tier or pre-frames the methodology for cold homepage traffic. Phase 3 builds a content layer with two jobs: warm the cold homepage visitor for the new VSL, and qualify the top 5-8% of warm followers into the Inner Circle waitlist.

Content Pillars

Five pillars. Each pulls a different segment of the 69K toward a clear next step.

Pillar 1 - The Methodology Story (Inner Circle feeder)

The "5 Simple Steps to Trading" methodology, told story-by-story. Not the steps themselves (those are inside the courses) - the why behind each step. The trades that taught her each rule, the working-adult constraint that shaped each rule, the beginner-mistake that each rule prevents.

Cadence: 1 long-form (8-12 min YouTube or carousel) per week + 3 short-form cuts.

CTA: "If the methodology lands and you want the room where I work it with operators 1:1, the Inner Circle waitlist opens [date]."

Pillar 2 - The Working-Adult Trader (Level 1 + masterclass feeder)

The day-job trader. The mom-with-three-kids trader. The 9-to-5 trader. Posts that name the working-adult constraint directly and show how the methodology bends to fit the schedule, not the other way around.

Cadence: 2 short-form / week + 1 carousel + 1 IG story series / week.

CTA: "Free masterclass walks the methodology. Link in bio."

Pillar 3 - The Anti-Pump-Group (differentiator content)

The trader-Twitter and Discord-pump-group reality - what is wrong with the noise, why the methodology beats the noise, and what working adults specifically should not be doing on their phone at 9:30am. This is the "differentiator" pillar that sets up the Inner Circle as the antidote to the noise.

Cadence: 1 long-form / week + 2 short-form cuts.

CTA: routes to homepage VSL or free masterclass depending on platform.

Pillar 4 - The Graduate Wins (proof pillar)

Real student stories. The working adult who hit a flat $500/day on Labs 1. The mom who built a Money Meeting routine for her family. The Discord regular who finally took the bundle. Permission-cleared, named where possible.

Cadence: 1 long-form / week (founder-narrated) + 2 short-form cuts.

CTA: "Same methodology. Free masterclass below."

Pillar 5 - The Methodology Behind The Money Meeting (cross-vertical)

The Money Meeting product is its own vertical (family wealth) and currently has no cross-sell into the rest of the ladder. Pillar 5 bridges it - the trader who teaches her own family the same methodology she teaches her students. This pillar warms the cross-sell into Inner Circle (family-wealth on-ramp).

Cadence: 1 short-form / week + 1 carousel / month.

CTA: Money Meeting course landing → Inner Circle waitlist via post-purchase nurture.

Inner Circle Pre-Launch Content (Weeks 1-4 post-kickoff)

The Inner Circle does not launch from a single post. It launches from a 4-week pre-frame:

  • Week 1: "What I would build for the operator past the bundle" (Pillar 1, long-form)
  • Week 2: "The one room I have not built yet" (Pillar 4, story-driven, hint at Inner Circle)
  • Week 3: "Here is what the Inner Circle looks like" (Pillar 1 + Pillar 4, formal reveal, waitlist open)
  • Week 4: "Last call for the founding cohort" (Pillar 1, urgency + scarcity, waitlist closes)

Cadence on launch week: daily IG, 3x YT shorts, 2x carousel, 1x long-form, plus a dedicated email broadcast.

Homepage VSL Pre-Launch (Weeks 1-2 post-kickoff)

The homepage VSL is mostly a recut of the existing free masterclass. Pre-launch content seeds the audience that the homepage is changing:

  • Day -7: "I am rebuilding the homepage" (curiosity)
  • Day -3: "Here is what is going on the new homepage" (preview, behind-the-scenes still)
  • Day 0: "New homepage live - watch the methodology in 5 minutes" (link to home.thebusytraders.com)

Repurpose Ratio

1 filmed asset = 1 long-form (YouTube or carousel) + 4-6 short-form cuts (IG reels + YT shorts) + 1 email broadcast + 1 newsletter section.

Target output: 5 short-form / week + 1 long-form / week + 1 email / week. Total 7 deliverables / week off 1-2 filming sessions / month.

Conversion Goal

Every piece of content has the same final-CTA hierarchy:

  1. Top intent (Pillar 1, Pillar 4): Inner Circle waitlist link
  2. Mid intent (Pillar 2, Pillar 5): Free masterclass opt-in (warms toward Level 1 via Bottleneck #3 nurture)
  3. Cold intent (Pillar 3): Homepage VSL (warms toward Level 1 directly)

Linktree or bio router updated to surface the highest-priority CTA based on cohort week (Inner Circle launch weeks override the default).
