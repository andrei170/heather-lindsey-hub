Three Bottlenecks I Found Inside Your Funnel - Heather Lindsey

All three install in less than 7 days from kickoff. Built to compound. Order by impact.

Snapshot

  • ~69K IG @thebusytrader + free "$500 A Day Trading Stocks" masterclass + Discord chat community
  • Public ladder: Level 1 ($624) → Level 2 ($724) → Labs 1 ($824) → Labs 2 ($824) → Bundle ($2,996)
  • Side products: Money Meeting ($324), Think or Swim Advanced Script (purchasable)
  • 1:1 coaching exists but is gated behind the member portal, no public price, no positioning, no waitlist
  • Backend on Kartra (heatherlindsey.kartra.com), checkout works, course delivery works
  • The structural gaps are not in the courses. They are in the layers above and around them.

Bottleneck #1 - No high-ticket mentorship tier above the $2,996 bundle

Snapshot: Bundle tops out at $2,996 for the four courses. Beyond that, the only listed coaching options are 1:1 sessions (30-min, 1-hr, 2-week, 30-day bootcamp) gated behind the member portal with no public price, no public positioning, no waitlist. The top 5-8% of bundle buyers and 30-day bootcamp graduates have nowhere to upgrade to.

Asymmetry: A done-with-you Inner Circle at $15K-$25K per year with quarterly retreats and a small private cohort would convert that top tier into recurring high-ticket revenue. Same audience. Same teacher. Same methodology. New room.

Why it is costing her: - Top-tier students who finish Labs 2 and want more have to either repeat content or leave the brand for someone else's mastermind - Discord chat is the only post-purchase community, so the most committed students lack a higher-touch room - 1:1 coaching is gated and priced privately, which means cold-traffic and warm leads cannot self-select up the ladder - Course revenue is one-shot. Mentorship revenue is recurring. The compounding layer is missing.

The fix: - "The Busy Trader Inner Circle" - $15K-$25K per year, capped private cohort - Quarterly in-person retreats, monthly group calls with Heather, weekly methodology accountability - Application-only entry, route off the bundle and the 30-day bootcamp graduates as the primary feeder - Public landing page with hint pricing, application form, waitlist mechanic so cohorts cap

Estimated impact: 8-12 buyers per year x $20K = $160K-$240K incremental Year-1, recurring. Compounds with every cohort added.

Why this is #1: Highest ceiling, highest margin, recurring revenue, and the warmest possible audience (bundle buyers who already trust the methodology) is the feeder pool. This is the asymmetric play.

Bottleneck #2 - Homepage has no public VSL pre-framing the methodology

Snapshot: The home.thebusytraders.com landing leads with the course grid + "5 Simple Steps" copy. The free $500 / day masterclass is the only video asset, and it sits behind an opt-in. Cold traffic landing on the homepage hits the course grid first, with no video walking through the methodology, the differentiator, or the founder's story.

Asymmetry: A 4-7 minute unguilded VSL on the homepage above the course grid (positioning why the 5-step methodology beats the noise of trader-Twitter and Discord-pump groups) lifts cold-traffic to Level 1 conversion meaningfully.

Why it is costing her: - A homepage without a video asks the cold visitor to read text and trust a $624 entry course on first contact - The methodology is the differentiator, but the methodology is currently locked behind the opt-in masterclass - Working adults who land from IG, search, or word of mouth have no fast way to size Heather as a real teacher before they hit the course grid - The free masterclass already exists. A 4-7 minute unguilded cut for the homepage is a recut, not a from-scratch production.

The fix: - 4-7 minute homepage VSL above the course grid - Cold open: who Heather is in 30 seconds, the methodology in 90 seconds, the differentiator (working-adult, methodology-first, anti-pump-group) in 120 seconds, the proof (graduate wins), the soft close to Level 1 or the free masterclass - Static poster frame so the page loads fast on mobile - Repurpose the masterclass footage where possible to keep filming overhead near zero

Estimated impact: Assuming current homepage converts at 1-2%, a VSL typically lifts to 3-5% on warm coaching and trading traffic. At Heather's audience size, that is $40K-$120K per year of recovered revenue from the same traffic.

Why this is #2: Cheaper to build than the Inner Circle, faster to install, and lifts the conversion rate of every other surface that points back to the homepage. Foundational lift on existing infrastructure.

Bottleneck #3 - No observable email nurture between free masterclass attendance and Level 1 purchase

Snapshot: The masterclass collects emails (gated opt-in), but no public-facing post-attendance sequence is referenced in the funnel. Industry norm in trading education is an 8-touch sequence over 14 days that lifts free-to-paid by 2-3x. The masterclass is the largest warm-list event in the funnel and currently has the smallest sequence supporting it.

Asymmetry: An 8-touch sequence over 14 days, segmented by attendance behaviour (live attended vs replay watched vs registered-no-show), routes the warm masterclass list straight into Level 1 with measured touches. Same audience. Same teacher. Better path.

Why it is costing her: - The masterclass is the highest-intent free event in the funnel - attendees are pre-qualified by their willingness to give 60+ minutes of attention - Without a sequence, the buying window closes inside 72 hours and the warm-list cools - The bundle exists, but bundle is rarely the first purchase. Level 1 is the right next step, and Level 1 is what the sequence should drive - Working adults need 5-8 touches to commit $624 - one masterclass + one purchase email is statistically thin

The fix: - 8-touch nurture, 14 days, segmented by attendance state - Touch architecture: T+0 (replay + summary), T+1 (the methodology in plain English), T+3 (the working-adult objection handled), T+5 (graduate story), T+7 (the differentiator vs trader-Twitter), T+9 (Level 1 framing - what is and is not in it), T+11 (last call + price hint), T+13 (decision frame, route to Level 1 or hold) - Segment routing: replay-watcher gets faster cadence, registered-no-show gets a "missed it, here is the recap" thread first - Measured against free-to-Level-1 conversion at day 14 and day 30

Estimated impact: If masterclass currently converts 2-3% to Level 1, an 8-touch sequence typically lifts to 5-7%. At masterclass volume, $30K-$80K per year recoverable. Fastest dollar-recovery lever in the package.

Why this is #3: Smallest ceiling of the three, but the easiest to install (no new asset filming required) and the closest to live revenue. Day-1 cash flow lever while Bottleneck #1 ramps.
