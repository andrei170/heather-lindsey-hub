Highest Revenue Lever
01

No High-Ticket Inner Circle Above The $2,996 Bundle

The bundle tops out at $2,996 for all four courses. Beyond that, the only listed coaching options are 1:1 sessions gated behind the member portal with no public price, no positioning, no waitlist. Your top 5-8% of bundle graduates and 30-day bootcamp completers have nowhere to upgrade. A done-with-you Inner Circle at $15K-$25K per year with quarterly retreats and a small private cohort would convert that top tier into recurring high-ticket revenue. Same audience. Same teacher. Same methodology. New room.

Cost of Waiting

~$13-20K/mo in recurring high-ticket revenue not collected. That compounds to ~$160-240K/yr in unbooked recurring revenue while bundle graduates either repeat content or leave for someone else's mastermind. Each cohort cycle missed = a year of compounding revenue you do not recover.

6 months from now ·~$80-120K never collected. Plus the next wave of bundle graduates with the same upgrade question and still no Inner Circle to point them to.

Fix · The Busy Trader Inner Circle. $15K-$25K/yr, capped cohort, application-only. Year-1 floor $160-240K recurring, compounds with every cohort added.
Fast Install
02

Homepage Has No VSL Pre-Framing The Methodology

The home.thebusytraders.com landing leads with the course grid + the "5 Simple Steps" copy. The free $500 / day masterclass is the only video asset, and it sits behind an opt-in. Cold traffic landing on the homepage has no fast way to size you as a teacher before they hit the price grid. A 4-7 minute homepage VSL above the course grid (positioning the methodology against trader-Twitter and Discord-pump-group noise) lifts cold-traffic conversion meaningfully on the same traffic.

Cost of Waiting

~$3-10K/mo in Level 1 sales not happening from the same homepage traffic - ~$40-120K/yr recoverable on existing visitor volume. Industry norm: a homepage VSL lifts cold-traffic conversion from 1-2% to 3-5% on warm coaching and trading traffic. Every week without the VSL = a week of cold visitors hitting a price grid with no pre-frame.

6 months from now ·~$20-60K never captured + an extra cohort of visitors who left without a methodology preview and never came back.

Fix · 4-7 minute homepage VSL above the course grid. Recut from existing masterclass footage as Day-1 placeholder, custom-recorded version live within 30 days. $40-120K/yr recovered revenue.
Compound Pickup
03

No Email Nurture Between Masterclass And Level 1

The free "$500 A Day" masterclass collects emails (gated opt-in), but no public-facing post-attendance sequence is referenced in the funnel. The masterclass is the highest-intent free event you run - attendees gave 60+ minutes of attention. Without a sequence, the buying window closes inside 72 hours and the warm-list cools. Industry norm in trading education: an 8-touch sequence over 14 days lifts free-to-paid by 2-3x.

Cost of Waiting

~$2-7K/mo in Level 1 sales never happening from the warm masterclass list - ~$30-80K/yr recoverable on existing masterclass volume. Working adults need 5-8 touches to commit $624. One masterclass + one purchase email is statistically thin. Each masterclass cohort that runs without nurture = a warm list that cools out without converting.

6 months from now ·~$15-40K never captured + several masterclass cohorts cooled out without a sequence to recover them.

Fix · 8-touch nurture, 14 days, segmented by attendance state (live / replay / no-show). Routes the warm masterclass list straight into Level 1. $30-80K/yr recovered, day-1 cash flow lever.
