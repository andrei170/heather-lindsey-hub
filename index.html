Highest Revenue Lever
01

No High-Ticket Inner Circle Above The $2,996 Bundle

The Bundle tops out at $2,996 for all four courses. Beyond that, the only listed coaching options are 1:1 sessions gated behind the member portal with no public price, no positioning, no waitlist. Your top 5-8% of Bundle graduates and 30-day bootcamp completers have nowhere to upgrade. A done-with-you Inner Circle at $15K-$25K per year, with quarterly retreats and a small private cohort, would convert that top tier into recurring high-ticket revenue. Same audience. Same teacher. Same methodology. New room.

Cost of Waiting

~$13-20K/mo in recurring high-ticket revenue not collected. That compounds to ~$160-240K/yr in unbooked recurring revenue while Bundle graduates either repeat content or leave for someone else's mastermind. Each cohort cycle missed equals a year of compounding revenue you do not recover.

6 months from now ·~$80-120K never collected. Plus the next wave of Bundle graduates with the same upgrade question and still no Inner Circle to point them to.

Fix · The Busy Trader Inner Circle. $15K-$25K/yr, capped cohort, application-only. Year-1 floor $160K-$240K recurring, compounds with every cohort added.
Fast Install
02

Homepage Has No VSL Pre-Framing The Methodology

home.thebusytraders.com leads with the course grid and the "5 Simple Steps" copy. The free "$500 A Day" masterclass is the only video asset, and it sits behind an opt-in. Cold traffic landing on the homepage has no fast way to size you as a teacher before they hit the price grid. A 4-7 minute homepage VSL above the course grid, positioning the methodology against trader-Twitter and Discord-pump-group noise, lifts cold-traffic conversion meaningfully on the same traffic.

Cost of Waiting

~$3-10K/mo in Level 1 sales not happening from the same homepage traffic. ~$40-120K/yr recoverable on existing visitor volume. Industry norm: a homepage VSL lifts cold-traffic conversion from 1-2% to 3-5% on warm coaching and trading traffic. Every week without the VSL equals a week of cold visitors hitting a price grid with no pre-frame.

6 months from now ·~$20-60K never captured, plus an extra cohort of visitors who left without a methodology preview and never came back.

Fix · 4-7 minute homepage VSL above the course grid. Recut from existing masterclass footage as Day-1 placeholder, custom-recorded version live within 30 days. $40K-$120K/yr recovered revenue.
Compound Pickup
03

No Email Nurture Between Masterclass And Level 1

The free "$500 A Day" masterclass collects emails (gated opt-in), but no public-facing post-attendance sequence is referenced in the funnel. The masterclass is the highest-intent free event you run, since attendees gave 60+ minutes of attention. Without a sequence, the buying window closes inside 72 hours and the warm list cools. Industry norm in trading education: an 8-touch sequence over 14 days lifts free-to-paid by 2-3x.

Cost of Waiting

~$2-7K/mo in Level 1 sales never happening from the warm masterclass list. ~$30-80K/yr recoverable on existing masterclass volume. Working adults need 5-8 touches to commit $624. One masterclass plus one purchase email is statistically thin. Each masterclass cohort that runs without nurture equals a warm list that cools out without converting.

6 months from now ·~$15-40K never captured, plus several masterclass cohorts cooled out without a sequence to recover them.

Fix · 8-touch nurture, 14 days, segmented by attendance state (live / replay / no-show). Routes the warm masterclass list straight into Level 1. $30K-$80K/yr recovered, day-1 cash flow lever.
Lowest-Friction Install
05

Speaker + Author Credibility Has No Capture Funnel

Your own public bio reads "Speaker, Author, Entrepreneur." Three named credibility surfaces, and none of them are observably wired into the email funnel. Speaker appearances, podcast guest spots, panel sessions, the audiences that listen to you for thirty to sixty minutes are the warmest audience you have, and they have no opt-in path into The Busy Trader catalogue today. The "Author" line on the brand surface does no funnel work either, no free first-chapter, no methodology-mapped author capture, no chapter sequence anchored on the writing. The credibility markers are on the brand surface. The capture layer underneath them is missing.

Cost of Waiting

~$1.5-5K/mo in Level 1 and Bundle enrolments not captured from speaker / podcast / author audiences who heard you and walked past with no opt-in path. ~$20K-$60K Year-1 floor on the speaker + author capture install, sourced from credibility surfaces that are already built. Every future appearance and every back-catalogue book sale that does not route into the email list is warm-audience inventory that does not compound.

6 months from now ·~$10-30K in Level 1 enrolments never captured, plus another six months of speaker rooms and podcast spots where the audience listened, nodded, and left without a way to keep going with you.

Fix · Two-part capture install. (1) Speaker / podcast appearance funnel: appearance-night opt-in page tracked by source + 14-day email sequence routing into the free masterclass and then Level 1. (2) Author funnel: free first-chapter download landing + chapter-mapped 14-day sequence + tier-routed CTA (Level 1 / Bundle / Inner Circle). If a book exists, both go live together. If not, the speaker funnel goes live first and the author funnel queues for whenever a signature piece of writing is ready.
Recurring Engagement
04

Existing Course IP Is Not Repurposed Onto YouTube, X, Or LinkedIn

You have ~69K on Instagram (@thebusytrader), an active Kartra-hosted course business, and a Discord community of paying students. The course catalogue (Level 1 through Labs 2, the Bundle, the Money Meeting) is already filmed. The free "$500 A Day" masterclass is already recorded. The Q&A archive is sitting in Kartra. Three channels where your ICP already shops for teachers are missing at audience tier. No observable long-form YouTube channel (the second-largest search engine, where trading-education buyers go to size a teacher before they pay). No X / Twitter presence (the swing-trader subculture lives on FinTwit, and female working-adult traders are an under-served voice in that space). No observable LinkedIn cadence at audience tier (the working-adult professional-woman segment is reachable but currently not reached). The teacher voice is already on tape. Only the redistribution is missing.

Cost of Waiting

~$3.5-10K/mo in YouTube + X + LinkedIn audience compounding into Level 1 ($624), the Bundle ($2,996), and free-masterclass opt-ins not happening. ~$40K-$120K Year-1 floor from an ongoing monthly engagement covering YouTube long-form (1-2x/week, repurposed from masterclass + Labs walkthroughs + Q&A archive), X long-form (2-3x/day, swing-trader register), and LinkedIn-light cadence (2-3x/week, working-adult-second-income narrative). All sourced from existing course IP. Zero new filming.

6 months from now ·Another six months of competing trading educators without your methodology depth out-publishing you on YouTube and FinTwit. The gap compounds. The professional-woman LinkedIn segment stays unreached.

Fix · Ongoing monthly engagement on creator distribution from your existing IP. YouTube long-form (1-2x/week, repurposed methodology + Labs walkthroughs + Q&A). X long-form (2-3x/day, swing-trader audience, your faith-warm methodology-led register). LinkedIn-light (2-3x/week, working-adult professional-woman narrative). Heather approves the calendar. AG1 writes, edits, and publishes. No new filming required.
