VSL Preview Script - Heather Lindsey

Phase 1 deliverable · SHORT preview only (4-5 minute outline) for the homepage VSL above the course grid. Recut from existing free masterclass footage where possible. Not an 8+ minute long-form script.

Format

4-5 minute homepage VSL hosted on YouTube unlisted (or Wistia for control), embedded above the course grid on home.thebusytraders.com. Static poster frame for fast mobile load. Click-to-play, no autoplay.

This is a SHORT preview cut, designed to pre-frame the methodology for cold homepage traffic and route the viewer into the free masterclass or Level 1 - not to replace the masterclass. The masterclass stays the long-form opt-in asset.

Cold Open (00:00 - 00:30)

On camera, Heather:

"I have been teaching working adults to trade the stock market for [N] years. The students who stick with me are the ones who already have a day job, a family, a calendar that does not bend for the market. The methodology I teach was built around that. Five simple steps, a 60-minute morning routine, no Discord pump groups, no signal services. If that sounds like the version of trading you have been looking for, this video is for you."

What The Methodology Is (00:30 - 01:30)

The 5 simple steps in plain English. No chart screenshots, no insider language. Walk the methodology at the level a working adult can follow on a phone screen on the bus to work.

  • Step 1: platform setup (Think or Swim / TD Ameritrade)
  • Step 2: the morning routine
  • Step 3: entry rules
  • Step 4: exit rules
  • Step 5: the journal habit

The point: the methodology is teachable. The point is not to teach it in 60 seconds. The point is to show that it is structured, not vibes-based.

Who This Is For (01:30 - 02:15)

Working adults. Day-job traders. Mom-with-three-kids traders. 9-to-5 traders who want a second income, not a career pivot. People who have looked at trader-Twitter and Discord-pump-groups and walked away because none of it fits a real adult life.

This is not for: full-time day-traders, signal-followers, "get rich in 30 days" buyers.

What Sets It Apart (02:15 - 03:00)

The differentiator: methodology over signals. The Busy Trader teaches; it does not tell you what to buy at 9:31am. Working adults need a repeatable system, not a daily reaction. The 5 steps are the system. The Discord chat is the community of people running the same system.

"Most people who fail at trading do not fail at picks. They fail at structure. The methodology is the structure."

The Path (03:00 - 03:45)

Quick walkthrough of the ladder:

  • Free masterclass: $500 A Day Trading Stocks - the methodology, deeper, free, gated by opt-in
  • Level 1 ($624): the install version - 5 simple steps, the platform setup, the first month of practice
  • Labs 1 + Labs 2 ($824 each): swing trading and continuation strategy
  • Bundle ($2,996): all four courses, save $500
  • Inner Circle (application): the room above the bundle, capped cohort

The point: there is a structured path. Pick the rung that matches where you are.

Close (03:45 - 04:30)

"Two doors below this video. The first is the free masterclass - 60 minutes, the methodology in depth, no cost. The second is Level 1 - $624, the install version, 5 lessons, ready to start this weekend. Both doors lead to the same place. Take the one that matches where you are. - Heather."

CTA below VSL: two buttons. - Primary: "Join The Free Masterclass" → existing opt-in - Secondary: "Start With Level 1" → existing Kartra checkout

Production Notes

  • Filming location: Heather's existing setup (desk, ring light, two-camera if available). One filming session, half a day.
  • Recut option: if filming is delayed, recut the existing free masterclass for a Phase 1 placeholder. Pull the cold open, the methodology walk, the differentiator section, and the close. Edit to 4-5 minutes. Replace with the custom recording inside the first 30 days of go-live.
  • Camera: main camera (Sony, Canon, or whatever Heather already uses for masterclass) + lavalier mic.
  • Wardrobe: branded Busy Trader colour palette where possible (yellow / black / white). Working-adult presentable, not overdressed.
  • Lighting: natural where possible; key light + soft fill for the desk shot.
  • Pace: target 1.0x watchable on a phone screen at all times. No long pauses. B-roll cuts every 20-30 seconds during the methodology walk.
  • Music: none under talking head. Light bed under B-roll cutaways only. Royalty-free, library-grade.
  • Captions: burnt-in for mobile autoplay-muted preview. SRT for the embedded long version.
  • Thumbnail (poster): Heather at desk, looking at camera, soft yellow brand backdrop. Title overlay: "The methodology in 5 minutes."
