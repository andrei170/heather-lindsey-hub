Email Sequences - Heather Lindsey

Phase 1 + 2 deliverable · 8-touch masterclass-to-Level-1 nurture (14 days), pre-call sequence, post-call sequence, bundle-graduate-to-Inner-Circle nurture.

Sequence 1 · Masterclass Nurture (8 touches, 14 days) - Bottleneck #3

Triggered when a new subscriber registers for the free "$500 A Day Trading Stocks" masterclass. Segmented by attendance state (live attended / replay watched / registered-no-show).

T+0 - Immediate (post-registration confirmation)

Subject: Your seat is saved - here is what to bring

Body: Confirmation, calendar invite link, the one ask ("bring a notepad - we work the methodology, you write the steps"), what to expect on the masterclass. Soft set-up: "After the masterclass, I will send you the next step. Watch your inbox."

Goal: confirm seat, lock attendance, set the expectation that the next step is coming.

T+1 hour after masterclass start - Live or replay branch

Subject (live attendees): You showed up - here is what we cover next Subject (replay watchers): You missed it live, here is the replay + what is next Subject (no-shows): Life happened - here is the replay so you do not lose the seat

Body (live): "You sat through the methodology. Here is the recap in three lines, and here is the next step." Three-bullet recap, soft Level 1 mention, link to homepage.

Body (replay): "Replay link below. Watch in the next 48 hours - the next email assumes you have seen it." Replay link, recap teaser, soft Level 1 mention.

Body (no-show): "Replay link below. No judgement, working adults have working-adult schedules. Watch when you can." Replay link, the methodology in three lines, "next email lands once you have watched."

Goal: route the three audiences into the right next-touch cadence without losing anyone.

T+1 day - The Methodology In Plain English

Subject: The 5 steps in plain English (no chart screenshots)

Body: Walk the 5-step methodology in everyday language. No jargon, no chart screenshots, no "look at this candle." This is the version you would explain to a sibling who has never traded. Closes with "Level 1 is the install version of this, walked step-by-step over 5 lessons. Link below if you want to look."

Goal: demonstrate the methodology is teachable to a working adult, not gated behind insider language.

T+3 days - The Working-Adult Objection

Subject: "I do not have time to watch the market all day"

Body: Address the most common working-adult objection head-on. The methodology is built for the trader with a day job - here is how the 5 steps fit a 60-minute morning routine. Specific time blocks, specific entry windows, specific exit rules. CTA → Level 1 page.

Goal: kill the time-objection before it kills the purchase.

T+5 days - The Graduate Story

Subject: [First name] - the version of you who started Level 1 last year

Body: A real graduate story, named where permission is granted, anonymised where not. Working adult, day job, family, started Level 1, walked the ladder, where they are now. Honest framing - not "I made $1M in my first month." More like "I built a routine I actually stick to, and here is what the first 90 days looked like." CTA → Level 1.

Goal: proof + identification. The reader recognises themselves in the story.

T+7 days - The Differentiator (anti-pump-group)

Subject: Why I do not run a Discord pump group (and why you should not follow one)

Body: Explicit anti-pump-group positioning. The difference between learning the methodology and following someone else's picks. Why the working adult specifically gets burned by signal-following services. The Busy Trader teaches; it does not signal. CTA → Level 1.

Goal: position Heather as the teacher, not the signal-shouter. Filter out the wrong-fit buyer before they purchase.

T+9 days - Level 1 Framing (what is and is not in it)

Subject: What is actually in Level 1 - and what is not

Body: Honest scope. Level 1 is the 5 simple steps + the platform setup + the first month of practice trades. It is not a "get rich in 30 days" course. It is the foundation. If you want the full methodology including swing trading and continuation strategies, that is Labs 1 and Labs 2. The bundle ($2,996) is the all-in route if you know you are going the distance. CTA → Level 1 page (primary), bundle page (secondary).

Goal: scope-set the buyer. Right-size the purchase to the buyer's intent.

T+11 days - Last Call (price hint, not price drop)

Subject: Quick check before I disappear from your inbox

Body: Two-line check-in. "Is Level 1 the right starting point for where you are at, or do you want me to point you somewhere else?" Soft segmentation: hot replies route to "great, link below"; warm replies get a 1:1 reply for triage; cold replies drop to the long-term broadcast list.

Goal: segment hot vs warm vs cold; route hot to purchase, warm to founder reply, cold to broadcast.

T+13 days - Decision Frame

Subject: Where you landed

Body: Clean ask: "Are we starting with Level 1, holding off, or pointing you somewhere else?" Three short options, three short links. No pressure. Clear decision frame. Either route triggers the right next sequence (course-onboarding, long-term nurture, or different-product recommendation).

Goal: close the 14-day window with a decision, route the rest to a 60-day re-engagement.

Sequence 2 · Pre-Call Nurture (3 touches before Inner Circle intake call)

Triggered when an Inner Circle Typeform applicant books the intake call.

T-7 days

Subject: Before our call - 3 questions I will ask you

Body: The three questions Heather will ask, in advance. Helps the applicant arrive prepared. Sets the room as a working session, not a sales pitch.

T-3 days

Subject: What I learned from the bundle graduates this year

Body: Story-driven. One specific "before-call" insight from a bundle graduate's path into the Inner Circle conversation. Sets the room.

T-1 day

Subject: Tomorrow at [time] - link inside, 1 thing to bring

Body: Calendar reminder, one specific prep ask (most-recent month's trade journal screenshot, current methodology adherence, one number from last quarter).

Sequence 3 · Post-Call Follow-Up (4 touches over 14 days)

Triggered after the Inner Circle intake call regardless of outcome.

T+1 hour - Thank You + Recap

Subject: Recap from our call + 2 next steps

Body: 1-paragraph recap, 2 specific action items Heather committed to, link to the agreed path (Inner Circle enrolment, waitlist, or referral elsewhere).

T+3 days - The Specific Resource

Subject: That [thing] I mentioned on the call

Body: Whatever specific resource Heather promised on the call (Money Meeting bonus chapter, Labs 1 module preview, Think or Swim Advanced Script setup, a podcast episode). Sent as a clean PDF or shared Notion link.

T+7 days - The Check-In

Subject: Quick - did you implement [X] yet?

Body: Soft accountability nudge. "Reply with a one-line update if you did. Reply with a question if you did not."

T+14 days - The Decision

Subject: Where you landed

Body: Clean ask: "Are we moving forward with Inner Circle for this cohort or holding for the next one?" No pressure. Clear decision frame. Either route triggers the right next sequence (cohort onboarding or 6-month re-engagement).

Sequence 4 · Bundle-Graduate-To-Inner-Circle Nurture (30 days, 5 touches)

Triggered when a buyer purchases the $2,996 bundle. Runs alongside the course delivery, never replaces it.

Day 0 - Welcome to the bundle

Subject: You bought the bundle - here is the room I built next

Body: Light touch. "You are starting Level 1 first. The Inner Circle is the room I built for you 6-12 months from now. I will mention it once or twice while you walk the ladder. Here is the page if you want to look now." Inner Circle landing link.

Day 14 - Mid-bundle check-in

Subject: Two weeks in - how is Level 1 landing?

Body: Founder check-in. Soft accountability ask. No Inner Circle mention.

Day 21 - The Inner Circle preview

Subject: Halfway through Level 1, here is what comes after Labs 2

Body: "Most bundle graduates ask the same question - 'what is next?' This is what is next. Inner Circle: capped cohort, twice a year, applications open [date]." Link to landing.

Day 28 - Application open reminder (cohort-timed)

Subject: Inner Circle applications - 14 days left

Body: Urgency + scarcity, real not manufactured. "Cohort caps at [N]. Applications close [date]. Link below if you want in."

Day 30 - Decision frame

Subject: Where you landed - Inner Circle or hold for next cohort

Body: Clean ask: "Apply for this cohort or hold for the next one?" Two options, two links. Hot replies route to Typeform; warm replies route to founder email reply for triage.
