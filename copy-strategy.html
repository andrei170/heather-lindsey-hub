Copy Strategy - Heather Lindsey

Foundation doc · Voice, framing, headline patterns for the new Inner Circle tier + masterclass nurture.

Voice

Working-adult-respectful, methodology-first, anti-hype. Heather teaches; she does not shout. The copy treats traders as adults with day jobs and family obligations - never as "lifestyle traders" or "side-hustle bros." Faith-aligned register optional, never the lead.

Use: trader, methodology, the 5 steps, Labs, Money Meeting, working adult, side income, busy schedule, the work, structure, repeatable, learn the platform, build the routine, week one, day one Avoid: game-changer, unlock, transform, blueprint, supercharge, abundance, manifest, secrets, hacks, quantum leap, journey, vibe, day-trade-rich, Lambo, lifestyle trader, financial freedom in 90 days, leverage (as verb), elevate, empower, dive deep, in today's fast-paced

Brand Pillars

  1. Methodology Over Tips - the 5 steps, Labs, Money Meeting are a system, not a content series. Every piece of copy should reinforce that there is a structured method, not a parade of one-off trades.
  2. Working Adult First - the audience has a day job, kids, a calendar that does not bend for the market. The copy bends to fit them, not the other way around.
  3. Anti-Pump-Group - explicit positioning against trader-Twitter, Discord-pump groups, "follow my picks" services. The Busy Trader teaches; it does not signal.
  4. Speaker / Author / Entrepreneur - Heather is a teacher, not a market-shouter. The brand reads as classroom, not casino.

Headline Bank

For the Inner Circle landing page, the homepage VSL framing, hub callouts, and email subject lines:

  • The room above the bundle. For traders who are ready for the next level of the methodology.
  • The Busy Trader Inner Circle - by application, capped cohort, twice a year.
  • You finished Labs 2. Here is what the next room looks like.
  • The methodology. The room. The cohort. Together for the first time.
  • A small private cohort of working-adult traders, twice a year, with quarterly retreats.
  • From bundle graduate to Inner Circle - the path most asked for, finally built.
  • Methodology-first. Working adult-first. Cohort-first. Inner Circle.
  • The 12-month version of what we do in the masterclass.

For the homepage VSL frame:

  • Five minutes on why the methodology beats the noise.
  • Watch the methodology in five minutes - then decide if Level 1 is your starting point.
  • The 5 simple steps, in five minutes, before you spend a dollar.

Framing for the Funnel

  • Top of funnel (cold IG, search, homepage VSL): "If you are a working adult who wants the methodology without the day-trade-rich nonsense, watch this."
  • Mid funnel (masterclass attendees, free email subscribers): "You watched the masterclass. Here is the next step - Level 1, $624, the 5 simple steps, ready to install on day one."
  • High funnel (bundle graduates, Discord regulars, 30-day bootcamp completers): "You have done the work. The Inner Circle is the room I built for you. Application below."

Inner Circle Positioning Stack (Bottleneck #1 the asymmetric play)

  1. Frame: the next room, not the next course. Mentorship over content.
  2. Differentiator: working-adult cohort (not full-time-trader cohort). Quarterly retreats designed around school-holidays + workweek calendars.
  3. Inclusion: monthly group calls with Heather, weekly accountability check-ins, quarterly in-person retreats, private community thread, methodology-update preview access.
  4. Cap: small (12-25 seats per cohort), twice per year, application-only. Scarcity is real, not manufactured.
  5. Pricing: hint only on cold surfaces (use "stage-specific, confirmed at intake" language). Explicit on the post-application call, on the hub-page if AG1-side, never on cold-traffic landing.
  6. Feeder: the bundle ($2,996) graduates and the 30-day bootcamp graduates are the warm pool. No paid acquisition for the first 12 months.

Banned Phrases

Universal AG1 ban list, plus Heather-specific: - No "blueprint" (overused in trading-coach niche) - No "secrets" or "hacks" (anti-pump-group positioning) - No "unlock" - No "transform" (unless paired with concrete trader metric) - No "first week of trading" income claims (regulatory + brand integrity) - No "passive income" framing (the methodology is active, not passive)

Voice Test

Every paragraph passes if a working adult reading it on the bus to work nods, not laughs. If it could appear in a generic "scale your business" course or a generic "passive income" funnel, rewrite it. The methodology is the brand; the methodology is the voice.
